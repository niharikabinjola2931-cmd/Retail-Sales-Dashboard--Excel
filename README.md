# Retail Sales Dashboard — Excel

## Overview
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing retail sales performance 
across regions, brands, categories and channels.

## KPIs
- Total Revenue · Total Units Sold
- Top Performing Region · Best Selling Brand
- Top Performing Category · Stock Sell-Through Rate %

## Charts
- 3 Year Revenue Trend (2022-2024)
- Total Delivered Units by Channel
- Units Sold by Pack Type
- Revenue per Region
- Which Products are Popular (Top 5 Brands)
- Category Performance Across Channels
- What they want to buy (Revenue by Category)

## Key Insights
- Yogurt dominates 41% of total revenue
- Only 11% stock sell-through rate across all regions
- Retail channel leads delivery volume
- All 3 regions perform consistently — no major outlier

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables · Slicers · Charts
- IFERROR · TEXT · ROUND formulas

## Dataset
Retail sales data — 3 years (2022-2024)
across 3 regions · 5 categories · 14 brands
